How to Read These Signals

RSI (Relative Strength Index)

Measures price momentum on a 0–100 scale:

  • • < 30 (Oversold): Panic selling / severe discount. Potential bounce zone.
  • • 30 – 70 (Neutral): Fair value range.
  • • > 70 (Overbought): Exuberant buying. High risk of pullback.

MACD (Trend Momentum)

Tracks trend acceleration:

  • • MACD > Signal: Bullish momentum taking over.
  • • MACD < Signal: Bearish momentum, sellers in control.
  • • Histogram Turn: The moment a trend flips direction.

Why 'HOLD' when Oversold?

The system avoids catching falling knives:

Even if an asset's RSI drops below 30, if the MACD momentum is still plummeting, the system triggers HOLD. It only fires BUY when multiple indicators align (RSI bottoming + MACD reversal + volume support).

Rule score vs hit rate

Two different numbers:

  • • Rule score: how many indicators agree. It is not a probability.
  • • Hit rate: how often this signal type was actually profitable after costs, measured on history. "Unproven" means too few cases to say.
  • • Edge vs long: did it beat simply holding? That is the number that matters.
