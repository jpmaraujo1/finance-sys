How to Read & Profit from These Signals

RSI (Relative Strength Index)

Measures price momentum on a 0–100 scale:

  • • < 30 (Oversold): Panic selling / severe discount. Potential bounce zone.
  • • 30 – 70 (Neutral): Fair value range.
  • • > 70 (Overbought): Exuberant buying. High risk of pullback.

MACD (Trend Momentum)

Tracks trend acceleration:

  • • MACD > Signal: Bullish momentum taking over.
  • • MACD < Signal: Bearish momentum, sellers in control.
  • • Histogram Turn: The moment a trend flips direction.

Why 'HOLD' when Oversold?

The system avoids catching falling knives:

Even if an asset's RSI drops below 30, if the MACD momentum is still plummeting, the system triggers HOLD to protect you. It only fires BUY when multiple indicators align (RSI bottoming + MACD reversal + volume support).
